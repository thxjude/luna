A little introduction

More than an assistant.
A familiar presence.

Luna is a personal AI assistant powered by OpenClaw. She runs on a privately managed home server and connects through Discord, bringing useful assistance into everyday conversations and projects.

Built around continuity, curiosity, and thoughtful interaction, Luna is designed to become more useful over time.

What Luna brings

Made to be useful.
